The boundary

Chipper Math gives no feeding or capacity verdicts. What a chipper can safely take is the manufacturer's call - follow the manual's capacity and safety instructions, and never force a branch the machine wasn't built for.

The pile truth

Sixteen-to-one is measured on pre-chipped air; the honest pile shrinks about 10:1 (labeled norm). Pile cubic feet divided by ten is the chips, divided by your bag is the bags - a single bag at the curb, a working row of paper bags, or a mulch bed's worth.

The weekend truth

Every branch costs honest minutes, and the thick ones cost six times the thin ones (labeled norms). Branch count times honest minutes against your free minutes is the real schedule: done before the charcoal is ready, spills past lunch, or a two-weekend pile.

What it never does

It never hands out a feeding verdict - what the machine can take is the manufacturer's call. It never counts the brochure's air as chips, never schedules the pile out of the weekend, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the last branch at dusk keeps the honest ledger.

Chipper Math is illustrative arithmetic with labeled norms, not a measurement of your chipper, your branches or your weekend. Follow the manual's capacity and safety instructions.
